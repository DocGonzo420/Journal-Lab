# Cover Lab

Make generative abstract art covers for notebooks at real print sizes, and save them as PNG or PDF.

**Open the app:** https://docgonzo420.github.io/Journal-Lab/cover-lab/

- **Styles:** Flow, Blocks, Contours, Layers, Terrazzo, Orbits, Waves, Halftone, Truchet, Shards, Plaid, Rays, Marble, Cutouts, each with endless variations from a seed (same seed, same art)
- **Colour:** 12 palettes, or mix your own six colours
- **Sizes:** Pocket, A5, A6, B6, Traveler's regular, Traveler's passport, Half letter, Letter, or a custom size
- **Print:** front only or wraparound (back + front), optional 3 or 5 mm bleed, label plate or band with your title
- **Save:** PNG or PDF at 150, 300 or 600 dpi, both at the real physical size, so printing at 100% comes out at the trim size plus any bleed

Cover Lab is its own app: it installs separately from Journal Lab (look for **Install app** at the bottom, or use your browser's Add to Home Screen) and works offline after the first visit. Its files live only in this `cover-lab/` folder, so it can be folded into Journal Lab later.

On an installed iPhone or iPad app, saving opens the share sheet (Save to Files, Save Image, Print), because installed apps there can't download files.
